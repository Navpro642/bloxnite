STORY MODE

Shoot a zombie until it drops. Walk up and press E to inject the cure. You have unlimited cures, and a saved zombie becomes a civilian again.

Bitten civilians can be cured too, but only for a few seconds before they turn.

Each level has a mission. Complete it to move on. Level 25 is the final boss.

ZOMBIE TYPES

Red ring: hunters. They come for you.

Orange ring: infectors. They go after civilians. Protect them.

Red arrows at the screen edge point to hunters behind you.

GAME MODES

Battle Royale: parachute in, loot, stay inside the storm. Most kills wins.

Free For All: everyone is an enemy. Most kills wins.

Team Deathmatch: red vs blue, most team kills wins.

Capture the Flag: grab their flag, bring it home. First to 3.

Search & Defuse: hold E at the orange site to plant or defuse. Sides switch after round 3.

Practice Range: 60 seconds, moving targets, your accuracy is measured.
